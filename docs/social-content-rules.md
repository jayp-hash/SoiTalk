# Soi Talk social content rules

Agreed with Jay on 23 Oct 2026. Check every reel and post against this list before it goes out.

**Motto: HOOK, retain, reward.** On social, the hook comes first and matters most.

## 1. The first 3 seconds decide everything
- **Why:** the first reel (22 Oct) had 152 views and reached 126 people, but only 40% of views got past 3 seconds. Average watch time was 6.4 s.
- **Open on the Thai moment itself:** a person or a speech bubble asking the question; the street, the stall, the counter.
- **Don't open on:** an intro, a logo, "Learn Thai with…", an English explanation, or a countdown.
- **It must work with the sound off.** On-screen text lands on the very first frame: the hook line plus Thai, romanization and English.
- **Use a pattern a viewer feels in a second:** a question they'll hear this week ("The 7-Eleven cashier asks this. What do you say?"), a mistake they're making, or a surprise ("Keys are counted like flowers in Thai").
- **Check before posting:** pause on frame 1 with the sound off. Would a Bangkok expat stop scrolling? If not, re-cut.

## 2. Retain
- **Length:** 12 to 15 s per reel.
- **Pacing:** the Thai answer by about 3 s, then the details.
- **No dead time:** no countdowns, no long end cards.
- **Loop:** the end cuts back to the opening frame.

## 3. Reward
- **One thing they can say today**, shown clearly at the end.
- **A reason to save:** "Save this for your next…", or a pinned cheat-sheet comment.
- **A light push:** a small soitalk.app tag throughout. The push goes in the caption and bio link, not a long end card.

## Always
- Both polite endings shown as chips: ครับ khráp (men), ค่ะ khâ / คะ khá (women).
- No unexplained Thai anywhere: every Thai line has romanization and English.
- At most 1 post in 4 is an app pitch.
- Every Thai line gets a native check before it is posted.
